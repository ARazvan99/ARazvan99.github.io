About

Engineering with a modern edge

I am a Geodetic Engineer with experience across surveying, cadastre, infrastructure design, civil engineering projects, public administration, funding-related projects, technical documentation, entrepreneurship, and AI-powered workflow automation.

My work combines field experience, office-based engineering, project coordination, digital tools, and business-oriented thinking. I am comfortable working both inside structured organizations and in entrepreneurial or B2B environments.

I combine traditional engineering with modern digital tools: surveying, engineering survey, cadastre, infrastructure design, GIS, GNSS, LiDAR and drone surveys, construction and civil engineering work — all documented through digital transformation, AI automation, workflow optimization, knowledge management and continuous learning.

I approach projects with a strong focus on quality, transparency, ownership, efficiency, and positive impact. Beyond delivering technical tasks, I look for opportunities to improve the surrounding workflow, documentation, collaboration, and organizational system.

In the office I focus on making every process I touch as efficient as possible and on delivering the best service I can. I use AI agents — Hermes, a VPS work PC and LLMs — as assistants. They support my work, they don't replace it: the success of the project comes first.
